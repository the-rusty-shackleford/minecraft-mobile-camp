# Real piston machinery, grounded structure, deployed controls and bedroll

Rusty requested clipping fixes, contact with the ground across the structure,
ordinary right-click module access through a separate block, and a bedroll that
does not change the player's respawn point. They then requested real automated
pistons and confirmed that the camp must remain one portable item, with the mod
coordinating storage and equipment around working pistons and module mounts.

This supersedes D-0002's prototype defaults of corner-only supports, packed-only
module access, ordinary bed respawn behavior and render-only moving geometry.
The single deployed camp limit, ownership, cargo retention, clear loaded terrain
and portable pickup remain.

The implementation keeps an 8x8 camp with six blocks of vertical clearance and
reserves an extra block on each side for temporary folding actuators (10x8 total).
Ten real vanilla piston strokes move three-block sections of the two wings,
layer by layer. Actual redstone blocks power the actuators. Vanilla moving-block
entities provide animation, sounds and motion persistence. The fixed rear sill
and center spine, supports and otherwise immovable stations are handled by the
prefab; chests and furnaces attach after the piston strokes.

Craftable structural parts and module mounts allow manual construction with
ordinary pistons. The module mount is a structural pad; portable inventory
storage and automatic station attachment belong to the prefab core. Manual
canvas placement permits rise/gable adjustment outside reserved camp sites.

The full deck reaches its block bottom; every foundation column bridges at most
three blocks. Model faces are partitioned or offset to eliminate coplanar surface
overlap. A blue control beside the red collapse switch opens a single deployed
module editor. Editing snapshots current station cargo before each change.
Wrong items and wrong bays are refused through actual slot rules. Collapse
requires the editor closed and the complete machinery volume clear.

The bedroll uses normal sleep rules but cancels its respawn update. Legacy camp
beds receive the same spawn protection. Ordinary beds and forced spawn commands
retain their behavior; unsafe-dimension use refuses without exploding.

Old deployed/in-flight camps keep their saved 0.1.0 transaction until packed.
Redeployment opts into the new mechanism and expanded preflight. New mechanism
state pauses without forced chunk loads, including while living occupants are
in the actuator strip. A cancelled or jammed stroke restores terrain and returns
the packed carrier with its cargo, rather than crashing or consuming it.

Rusty's additional rejected-placement report exposed client prediction consuming
a camp before server-only site validation. Placement now waits for server approval,
and rejection explicitly resends unchanged authoritative inventory, including
offhand and nested components.

Rusty explicitly authorized the combined release and requested a 120-second
warning before the server restart, superseding the earlier five-minute countdown.
