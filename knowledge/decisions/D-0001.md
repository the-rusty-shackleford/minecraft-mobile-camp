# Designed expedition shelter with attached modular equipment

Rusty requested a new mod named C.A.M.P. in `minecraft-mobile-camp`: a placeable block
that mechanically expands into a miniature campsite, with visible motion and sound
evoking elaborate transforming machinery.

They described a structure around 8 by 8 blocks, tentatively also 8 blocks high, with
a crafting station, furnace, double chest, bed and survival necessities. Its equipment
should be modular so that future content can enhance it. They chose a rugged expedition
appearance while retaining an impressive transformation.

For the layout, Rusty chose a mixture of an enclosed sleeping/storage section and a
covered outdoor workshop. Asked whether the camp should be a designed structure or
capture arbitrary player builds, they answered: "Designed shelter with modules".
Asked whether deployed components should be independently mineable, they answered:
"Things should be attached and can be changed via modules".

This fixes the product direction, not its implementation. Exact dimensions, attachment
interfaces, terrain rules, inventory transport and recovery details remain open. It does
not approve arbitrary block capture or independently harvestable shelter components.
No release is authorised by this design decision.
